About

Messy sensor data in.
Decisions out.

Most of my work sits at the point where raw data is still unusable and somebody needs an answer from it anyway.

At SIU Airport I work with aircraft flight logs — thousands of parameters sampled every second across every flight. I built the PySpark pipeline that ingests and cleans that data, the rule-based logic that flags skids, near-misses, hard landings and taxi-speed violations, and the BigQuery datasets feeding the Power BI dashboards the safety team actually reads.

Before that, four years at Accenture on the Travelers account, where I was the person on the other end of a P1 at 3am. That job taught me the part of engineering that doesn't show up in a repo: how to keep a system standing while you fix it, and how to explain what happened to someone who doesn't want a stack trace.

I'm finishing a Master's in Computer Science at Southern Illinois University Carbondale, and I'm currently going deeper on Java and Spring Boot full-stack work alongside the data side.
