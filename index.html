Full Stack Developer with over five years of experience across enterprise application development, cloud-native architecture, and production Generative AI systems.

My current work centres on designing and operating retrieval-augmented generation platforms at scale: multi-tenant ingestion and embedding pipelines, hybrid retrieval with re-ranking, and agentic orchestration incorporating tool calling and human-in-the-loop approval. These systems are delivered through streaming React and Next.js interfaces backed by Python and FastAPI services.

A consistent focus throughout is measurable quality. Retrieval performance is evaluated continuously against faithfulness, context precision, recall, and relevancy, while model routing, fallback logic, and quality gates keep inference cost and latency predictable under production load.

This work builds on four and a half years at Accenture delivering Java, Spring Boot, and React services within cross-functional Agile teams — optimising REST APIs with Redis and Apache Kafka, expanding automated test coverage, and managing deployments across Azure, Docker, and Kubernetes.

I work closely with Product, Security, and Engineering, which in practice means retrieval and access decisions are made alongside authentication, RBAC, and PII-handling constraints rather than retrofitted afterwards. I am currently completing a Master of Computer Science at Southern Illinois University, graduating December 2026.
